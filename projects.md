# Research Projects

## Poly(A) Tail Detection with Nanopore Data using Gaussian HMM
**University of Copenhagen** | November 2024 - January 2025

This project focused on improving the detection of polyadenylation tail regions in RNA sequencing data from Oxford Nanopore Technologies.

**Key contributions:**
- Analyzed nanopore sequencing data to identify poly(A) tail features
- Optimized detection algorithms by training a Gaussian Hidden Markov Model with curated training data
- Created a custom scoring function to evaluate prediction quality against Dorado baseline
- Demonstrated improved accuracy in tail length estimation for downstream analysis

**Technologies:** Python, hmmlearn, nanopore sequencing data analysis

## Age of Runs of Homozygosity
**University of Copenhagen** | June 2024 - August 2024

This population genetics project investigated the temporal dynamics of genomic regions with reduced diversity.

**Key contributions:**
- Assessed the correlation between ROH (Runs of Homozygosity) size and age through computational simulations
- Analyzed how demographic factors influence the distribution of ROH ages across populations
- Employed coalescent-based simulations to generate ancestral trees
- Identified ROH in sampled individuals and estimated their size and age distributions
- Provided insights into population history and inbreeding patterns

**Technologies:** Python, coalescent simulation tools, population genetics analysis

## Master Thesis: Benchmarking Prediction of Mutational Effects on Protein-Protein Interactions
**University of Copenhagen** | 2024 - 2025

This research evaluated computational methods for predicting how mutations affect protein-protein interactions using large-scale experimental data.

**Key contributions:**
- Compiled and processed MAVE (Multiplexed Assays of Variant Effects) datasets
- Benchmarked multiple prediction algorithms for accuracy and reliability
- Analyzed performance across different protein families and interaction types
- Provided recommendations for method selection based on use case

**Technologies:** Python, R, protein structure analysis tools, statistical modeling
