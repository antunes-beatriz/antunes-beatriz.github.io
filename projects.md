      <p class="company">Amelie Stein Group</p>
    </div>
    <div class="experience-meta">
      <span class="duration">June 2024 - August 2024</span>
      <span class="location">Copenhagen, Denmark</span>
    </div>
  </div>
  <p class="project-description">This population genetics project investigated the temporal dynamics of genomic regions with reduced diversity.</p>
  
  <h3 class="subsection-title">Key Contributions</h3>
  <ul class="experience-details">
    <li>Assessed the correlation between ROH (Runs of Homozygosity) size and age through computational simulations</li>

    <li>Employed coalescent-based simulations to generate ancestral trees</li>
    <li>Identified ROH in sampled individuals and estimated their size and age distributions</li>
    <li>Provided insights into population history and inbreeding patterns</li>
  </ul>
